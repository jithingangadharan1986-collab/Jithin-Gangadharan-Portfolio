PM Coach LIVE

An AI interview coaching agent that personalizes questions and scores your answers in real time.

The problem

Generic PM interview prep gives scripted feedback that has no idea what you've actually built — a PM who shipped a supply chain AI product gets treated identically to a fresh graduate. I was the user: I was prepping for exactly these interviews and the existing tools didn't reference my background at all.

What I built

I own the product decisions: the profile-first onboarding flow, the system prompt that injects a user's background into every call, the three-dimension scoring framework (Structure, Specificity, Impact), and the structured output format the UI parses and renders as live score bars. The LLM generates the text — I designed the product around it and what "good" means.

RecoverStrong LIVE

An AI recovery app built for post-cancer treatment patients.

The problem

Generic fitness and nutrition apps don't account for treatment cycles, and most nutrition guidance isn't built around Indian food — so recovering patients are left adapting advice that wasn't made for their situation.

What I built

An app that generates fitness and nutrition plans adapted to a patient's treatment cycle, built around Indian foods from the ground up rather than adapted after the fact.

SplitEasy LIVE

A real-time group expense splitter that doesn't cap out at 15–20 people.

The problem

GPay and PhonePe both cap group payment splits at 15–20 members, which breaks down fast for trips, shared households, or any larger group expense.

What I built

A group expense tracker with live sync across all members and direct UPI deep-link payments — so settling up doesn't require leaving the app or hitting a group-size wall.
